Likely cause of black mist edges in Nav2 + SLAM Toolbox
Walls are being written repeatedly with small pose offsets, so edges blur instead of staying sharp.
Main causes (priority order)


Odometry jitter/drift (most common)


Robot turning/moving too fast during LiDAR scan (motion distortion)


SLAM Toolbox tuning not tight enough


Map resolution too low


LiDAR noisy / reflective surfaces
